01 · Brand

Holidaze connects travellers with unique stays worldwide. The tone is friendly yet professional, with short, action-driven labels like “Book now” and “Get started”.

06 · Accessibility

  • All text meets WCAG AA; body and headings reach AAA.
  • Every interactive element has a visible focus ring (2px Primary 700, offset).
  • Colour is never the only signal — errors add text and aria-invalid.
  • Semantic HTML: <nav>, <main>, <footer>, headings in order.
  • Icon-only buttons carry aria-label; images carry meaningful alt.
  • A skip link, per-page titles and reduced-motion support are built in.
